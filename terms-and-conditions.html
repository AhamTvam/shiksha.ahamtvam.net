3. Courses Offered

AhámTvám Shiksha currently offers structured DaVinci Resolve video editing courses designed for different levels of learners.

Vaali — Beginner

The Vaali course is intended for beginners who want to understand the fundamentals of video editing and DaVinci Resolve.

The program covers four weekends and includes introduction and installation, editing foundations, transitions and audio, and basic colour correction and export.

Sugreeva — Intermediate

The Sugreeva course is intended for students who already understand basic editing and want to develop stronger editing, storytelling, motion, and workflow skills.

The program covers six weekends, including the foundational modules followed by advanced editing workflow and storytelling and motion basics.

Garuda — Advanced

The Garuda course is intended for students seeking advanced and professional-level editing knowledge.

The program covers eight weekends, including the foundational and intermediate modules followed by professional editing systems, effects, freelancing, and career development topics.
