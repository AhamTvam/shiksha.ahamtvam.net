About This Course

DaVinci Resolve — Garuda (Advanced) is an advanced live online video editing course designed for editors who want to develop professional workflows, advanced creative techniques, and a stronger understanding of commercial post-production using DaVinci Resolve.

This 8-weekend live training program focuses on taking video editing beyond basic production into professional editing systems, advanced storytelling, motion graphics, cinematic effects, and commercial workflows. Students will explore how professional editors manage projects involving multiple video and audio layers while improving editing speed, sequencing, pacing, and audience engagement.

The program also introduces professional systems and effects, including motion graphics, speed ramping, cinematic transitions, and professional color grading. These skills are combined with practical knowledge of client acquisition, pricing strategy, portfolio building, and personal branding for editors.

By completing the Garuda Program, learners develop a broader understanding of professional video editing workflows and the skills required to work with agencies, brands, creators, and freelance clients while building a sustainable editing career.

Course Curriculum

The Garuda (Advanced) program follows a structured 8-weekend learning journey covering foundation, intermediate, and advanced professional video editing skills using DaVinci Resolve. The program progresses from editing fundamentals to professional workflows, cinematic techniques, and career development.

Weekend 1 — Introduction & Installation

Build the foundation required to work confidently with DaVinci Resolve by understanding the editing industry, software setup, workspace, and timeline workflow.

  • Introduction to Video Editing Industry
  • Software Installation & Setup
  • Workspace Overview
  • Timeline Basics

Weekend 2 — Editing Foundations

Learn the core editing techniques required to create clean sequences, organize projects efficiently, and develop a basic storytelling flow.

  • Cuts & Trims
  • Sequencing Techniques
  • Clip Organization
  • Basic Storytelling Flow

Weekend 3 — Transitions & Audio

Develop essential creative and audio editing skills by working with transitions, synchronized audio, music placement, and volume balancing.

  • Transitions & Effects
  • Audio Syncing
  • Music Placement
  • Volume Balancing

Weekend 4 — Color Grading & Export

Complete the foundation stage by learning basic color correction, LUT usage, professional export settings, and social media optimization.

  • Basic Color Correction
  • LUT Usage
  • Export Settings
  • Social Media Optimization

Weekend 5 — Advanced Editing Workflow

Move into professional editing workflows by working with multiple video and audio layers, improved pacing, advanced sequencing, and optimized editing processes.

  • Multi-layer Editing
  • Editing Pacing
  • Advanced Sequencing
  • Workflow Optimization

Weekend 6 — Storytelling & Motion Basics

Strengthen your creative editing skills through visual storytelling, animated text, overlays, and techniques designed to improve audience engagement.

  • Visual Storytelling
  • Text Animation
  • Overlay Effects
  • Audience Engagement

Weekend 7 — Professional Systems & Effects

Explore professional commercial editing systems and advanced creative techniques used to produce polished and cinematic video content.

  • Commercial Editing Workflow
  • Motion Graphics
  • Speed Ramping & Effects
  • Professional Color Grading

Weekend 8 — Freelancing & Career Growth

Develop the professional skills required to position yourself as a video editor, attract clients, and build a sustainable freelance career.

  • Client Acquisition
  • Pricing Strategy
  • Portfolio Building
  • Personal Branding
