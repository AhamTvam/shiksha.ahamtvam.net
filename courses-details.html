About This Course

Begin your journey into professional video editing with Vaali, AhámTvám Shiksha's beginner-friendly DaVinci Resolve Course. Designed for complete beginners, this live online video editing course provides a structured learning experience that helps you build a strong foundation in editing techniques, creative storytelling, and post-production workflows.

Throughout the program, you'll learn how to confidently use DaVinci Resolve—from installing the software and understanding its interface to organizing projects, performing clean edits, synchronizing audio, applying transitions, correcting colors, and exporting videos for platforms like YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and other digital media.

Unlike self-paced tutorials, Vaali focuses on live instructor-led learning with practical assignments, real-world editing exercises, and continuous mentor guidance. Every concept is taught step by step, making it ideal for students, aspiring content creators, freelancers, entrepreneurs, and anyone looking to build professional video editing skills from scratch.

By the end of this program, you'll be able to independently edit complete videos using industry-standard workflows, understand the fundamentals of visual storytelling, and confidently create content suitable for social media, marketing, personal branding, and digital businesses. Successful learners will receive an official AhámTvám Shiksha Course Completion Certificate and become eligible to continue their journey with the Sugreeva Program.

Course Curriculum

The Vaali Program follows a structured 4-weekend live learning journey designed to help complete beginners build a strong foundation in professional video editing using DaVinci Resolve. Each weekend introduces new practical concepts that gradually develop your editing skills through live demonstrations, guided exercises, and real-world workflows.

Weekend 1 — Introduction & Workspace Setup

Begin your journey by understanding the video editing industry, installing DaVinci Resolve, exploring the workspace, and learning how professional editing projects are organized.

  • Introduction to Industry
  • Software Installation & Setup
  • Workspace Overview
  • Timeline Basics

Weekend 2 — Editing Fundamentals

Learn the essential editing techniques used in professional productions, including clean cuts, efficient sequencing, media organization, and creating a logical storytelling flow.

  • Cuts & Trims
  • Sequencing Techniques
  • Clip Organization
  • Basic Storytelling Flow

Weekend 3 — Audio & Creative Editing

Discover how professional editors improve the viewing experience through transitions, synchronized audio, music placement, and balanced sound that enhances every project.

  • Transitions & Effects
  • Audio Syncing
  • Music Placement
  • Volume Balancing

Weekend 4 — Color Correction & Export

Complete your foundation by learning basic color correction, LUT workflows, professional export settings, and preparing videos for YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and other digital platforms.

  • Basic Color Correction
  • LUT Usage
  • Export Settings
  • Social Media Optimization
