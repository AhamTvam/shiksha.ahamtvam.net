About This Course

DaVinci Resolve — Vaali (Basic) is designed for beginners who want to build a strong foundation in professional video editing. Through live weekend classes, practical assignments, and real-world projects, you'll learn how to confidently edit videos using DaVinci Resolve.

Whether you're a student, aspiring freelancer, content creator, or working professional, this course provides the essential skills needed to produce high-quality videos for YouTube, social media, corporate projects, and personal portfolios.


What You'll Learn

  • DaVinci Resolve Interface & Workflow
  • Project Setup & Media Management
  • Timeline Editing
  • Cutting & Trimming Techniques
  • Audio Editing & Sound Balancing
  • Titles & Basic Motion Graphics
  • Color Correction Fundamentals
  • Export Settings for YouTube, Instagram & Professional Delivery

Learning Outcomes

  • Edit professional-quality videos from scratch.
  • Create engaging content for YouTube and social media.
  • Perform clean cuts, transitions, and basic motion effects.
  • Apply professional audio balancing techniques.
  • Perform basic color correction and grading.
  • Export videos in the correct formats.
  • Build your first professional editing portfolio.

Who Should Join?

  • Beginners with no prior editing experience.
  • Students looking to develop creative skills.
  • YouTubers & Content Creators.
  • Freelancers and aspiring video editors.
  • Working professionals exploring creative careers.

Course Curriculum

This course is structured across 4 weekends (8 live sessions), providing a step-by-step learning experience from the fundamentals of video editing to creating complete professional projects.

Weekend 1 – Getting Started with DaVinci Resolve

  • Introduction to Video Editing
  • Installing DaVinci Resolve
  • Understanding the User Interface
  • Project Creation & Settings
  • Importing & Organizing Media
  • Timeline Basics

Weekend 2 – Professional Editing Techniques

  • Cutting & Trimming
  • Ripple, Roll & Slip Editing
  • Transitions
  • Titles & Text
  • Basic Motion Effects
  • Editing Workflow Best Practices

Weekend 3 – Audio & Color Fundamentals

  • Audio Editing
  • Sound Balancing
  • Background Music
  • Basic Color Correction
  • Primary Color Wheels
  • Matching Multiple Clips

Weekend 4 – Export & Final Project

  • Export Settings
  • Rendering for YouTube & Instagram
  • Optimizing Video Quality
  • Final Practical Project
  • Portfolio Building Tips
  • Career Guidance & Next Steps
