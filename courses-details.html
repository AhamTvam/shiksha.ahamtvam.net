About This Course

The DaVinci Resolve – Sugreeva (Intermediate) program is designed for learners who have already mastered the basics of video editing and are ready to develop professional editing skills. This live online weekend course focuses on refining editing workflows, improving storytelling techniques, mastering motion elements, enhancing audio, and creating cinematic visuals through professional color grading.

Conducted over 6 weekends with 90-minute live classes, the program combines guided instruction with practical exercises to help students work more efficiently and confidently using DaVinci Resolve. Every module builds on the previous one, allowing learners to strengthen both their technical skills and creative decision-making.

By the end of the course, students will be able to edit smoother, more engaging videos, apply advanced editing workflows, improve pacing and sequencing, create professional motion elements, enhance audio quality, perform cinematic color grading, and export videos optimized for different platforms.

Course Curriculum

The Sugreeva (Intermediate) Program is delivered over 6 weekends through live online classes. Each weekend focuses on a specific stage of professional video editing, helping learners progress from editing foundations to advanced workflow optimization and cinematic storytelling.

Week 1 — Introduction & Installation

  • Introduction to Industry
  • Software Installation & Setup
  • Workspace Overview
  • Timeline Basics

Week 2 — Editing Foundations

  • Cuts & Trims
  • Sequencing Techniques
  • Clip Organization
  • Basic Storytelling Flow

Week 3 — Transitions & Audio

  • Transitions & Effects
  • Audio Syncing
  • Music Placement
  • Volume Balancing

Week 4 — Color Grading & Export

  • Basic Color Correction
  • LUT Usage
