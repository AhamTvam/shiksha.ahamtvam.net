About This Course

DaVinci Resolve — Vaali (Basic) is a beginner-friendly live online video editing course designed for students, aspiring content creators, freelancers, and anyone who wants to learn professional video editing using DaVinci Resolve.

This 4-weekend live training program introduces you to the fundamentals of video editing through a structured and practical learning experience. You will learn how to set up DaVinci Resolve, understand its workspace, manage media, work with timelines, perform clean cuts and trims, organize clips, and build a basic storytelling flow.

As you progress through the program, you will also learn essential transitions and effects, audio syncing, music placement, volume balancing, basic color correction, LUT usage, export settings, and video optimization for social media platforms.

The course combines live instruction with practical exercises to help you understand the complete video editing workflow and develop the confidence to create and export your own videos using DaVinci Resolve.

Course Modules

The Vaali Program follows a structured 4-weekend learning journey designed to build a strong foundation in video editing using DaVinci Resolve.

Weekend 1 — Introduction & Workspace Setup

Begin by understanding the video editing industry and setting up DaVinci Resolve. Learn the workspace and the fundamentals of working with a video editing timeline.

  • Introduction to Industry
  • Software Installation & Setup
  • Workspace Overview
  • Timeline Basics

Weekend 2 — Editing Fundamentals

Learn the fundamental techniques required to assemble and organize video footage while developing a basic storytelling flow.

  • Cuts & Trims
  • Sequencing Techniques
  • Clip Organization
  • Basic Storytelling Flow

Weekend 3 — Transitions & Audio

Learn how transitions, music, and synchronized audio contribute to a smoother and more engaging video editing experience.

  • Transitions & Effects
  • Audio Syncing
  • Music Placement
  • Volume Balancing

Weekend 4 — Color & Export

Complete your foundation by learning basic color correction, LUT usage, export settings, and preparing videos for social media platforms.

  • Basic Color Correction
  • LUT Usage
  • Export Settings
  • Social Media Optimization
