About This Course

DaVinci Resolve — Vaali (Basic) is a comprehensive live online beginner video editing course designed for students, content creators, aspiring freelancers, business owners, and anyone who wants to build a professional foundation in video editing using DaVinci Resolve.

This 4-weekend live training program focuses on practical learning, helping students understand the complete editing workflow—from software installation and workspace setup to professional editing techniques, audio enhancement, color correction, and final video delivery. Every concept is taught through live demonstrations, guided practice, and real-world editing exercises to ensure confidence with every tool and workflow.

Whether you're planning to start a career in video editing, become a freelance editor, grow your YouTube channel, or create engaging content for social media and businesses, this course provides the perfect starting point to develop industry-ready editing skills.
