About This Course

DaVinci Resolve — Sugreeva (Intermediate) is designed for learners who have mastered the fundamentals of video editing and are ready to advance their skills with professional editing workflows. This live online intermediate program focuses on cinematic storytelling, motion graphics, advanced audio enhancement, and professional color grading techniques used in real-world post-production.

Delivered over 6 weekends of live interactive sessions, the course emphasizes practical learning through hands-on projects and industry-oriented workflows. Students will work with advanced editing techniques, visual effects, text animation, sound design, and cinematic finishing while developing the confidence to handle commercial editing assignments.

Whether your goal is to become a freelance video editor, create professional content for brands, or prepare for advanced post-production, the Sugreeva Program bridges the gap between basic editing knowledge and industry-level creative execution.
