Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains the conditions and procedure for requesting refunds or cancelling an enrollment for courses offered by AhámTvám Shiksha.

Effective Date: 11 October 2026

Last Updated: 11 October 2026

1. Introduction

AhámTvám Shiksha is an educational initiative operated by AHAMTVAM WORLD PRIVATE LIMITED ("AhámTvám", "we", "us", or "our").

This policy applies to paid courses and related educational services purchased through our website or other authorised enrollment channels.

Please read this policy before completing your enrollment. Refund requests will be reviewed according to the conditions below and applicable law.

2. Student-Requested Refunds: 24-Hour Window

A student requesting a refund for a change of mind or another personal reason must submit the request within 24 hours of the registration or subscription transaction.

The request must be sent to support.atshiksha@gmail.com within this period.

The student must provide accurate information sufficient for us to identify and verify the enrollment and payment. This should include:

  • Student's full name.
  • Registered email address and phone number.
  • Registration ID, if available.
  • Course name and selected package.
  • Payment ID or transaction reference.
  • Payment date, amount, and a clear reason for the request.

We may request additional information reasonably necessary to verify the request. Students should not send passwords, UPI PINs, CVVs, or other payment authentication credentials.

Submitting a request within 24 hours does not automatically guarantee approval. Each request will be reviewed against this policy and applicable legal requirements.

3. Duplicate Payments

If a student is charged more than once for the same enrollment because of a duplicate transaction, the duplicate payment is eligible for a refund after verification.

The student should provide the relevant transaction references and registration details so we can identify the duplicate charge.

A valid duplicate-payment refund request is not subject to the standard 24-hour student-requested cancellation window. It will be reviewed and processed in accordance with applicable requirements.

4. Failed Payments and Unsuccessful Enrollment

If a payment fails or an enrollment is not completed but money has been debited from the student's account, we will review the transaction and its status with the payment service provider.

Where the payment was not successfully completed and the amount is confirmed as refundable, the reversal or refund will be handled through the relevant payment process.

A pending transaction, payment failure message, or delayed bank update may not mean that the amount has been finally collected by us. The transaction will be checked before a separate refund is initiated, to avoid duplicate refunds.

5. Qualifying Exceptional Cases

We may approve a refund outside the standard student-requested cancellation process where the circumstances reasonably justify it, subject to applicable law.

Qualifying cases may include:

  • Cancellation of a course by AhámTvám where an appropriate replacement or rescheduled option is not accepted or cannot reasonably be provided.
  • A material failure to provide the paid course or service as described, where a refund is an appropriate remedy.
  • A verified billing or payment-processing error.
  • Other exceptional circumstances approved after review.

Each case will be assessed individually. We may ask for supporting information necessary to establish what occurred. Any refund required by applicable law will be handled in accordance with that law.

6. Course Cancellation or Rescheduling by AhámTvám

If AhámTvám Shiksha cancels a scheduled course or is unable to provide the purchased course, we will communicate the available options to affected students.

Depending on the circumstances, these options may include rescheduling, an appropriate alternative course arrangement, or a refund where applicable.

If a student does not accept a proposed alternative and qualifies for a refund, the refund will be reviewed and processed accordingly.

7. When a Refund May Not Be Approved

Subject to applicable law and the exceptions described in this policy, a student-requested refund may be declined where:

  • The request is submitted after the 24-hour window and no qualifying exception applies.
  • The enrollment and payment details cannot be verified after reasonable attempts to obtain clarification.
  • The request does not qualify under the applicable refund conditions.

A request will not be rejected solely because it falls outside the standard 24-hour window if a refund is otherwise required under applicable law or qualifies under the exceptional circumstances in this policy.

8. How to Request a Refund

Send your request by email to support.atshiksha@gmail.com with the subject line:

Refund Request — [Registration ID]

Include the student and transaction details listed in Section 2, along with a clear explanation of the request.

We may contact you using your registered email address or phone number to verify the information or request additional details.

9. Review and Processing of Refunds

We will review the request and communicate the outcome using the contact information provided by the student.

Once a refund is approved, it will ordinarily be initiated through the original payment method or the applicable payment-provider process, where supported.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the student's account may depend on the payment provider, bank, card issuer, or other financial institution. We will provide relevant information about the refund where available.

Refunds will be handled within the time required by applicable law or, where no specific period applies, within a reasonable period after approval and completion of the required verification.

10. Course Access Following a Refund

Where a refund is approved and completed, access to the refunded course or associated student services may be withdrawn or cancelled, as appropriate.

Students must not continue using paid course materials or access that has been withdrawn following a completed refund.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our educational services, enrollment process, payment arrangements, or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. The applicable law will prevail over any inconsistent provision in this policy.

For refund requests, cancellation enquiries, or questions about this policy, please contact us using the details below.
